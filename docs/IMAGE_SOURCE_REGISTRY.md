# Image Source Registry

Accessed 2026-07-30. All assets are bundled locally; there is no runtime hotlinking. OpenMoji SVGs retain their upstream 72×72 viewBox and are already compact vector files. “Clinical review” means the asset was screened for fit with existing educational text, not independently approved by a clinician.

## Rejected candidates

- Wikimedia “Obstruction ventilation apnée sommeil”: licensing was clear, but accuracy concerns appeared in the Commons review; rejected.
- Low-resolution/product-specific CPAP photos and mask images: rejected for visual inconsistency and implied product emphasis.
- General lung diagrams: rejected because OSA teaching here concerns the upper airway and the visual could misdirect attention.
- Outcome-specific gestational diabetes or preeclampsia imagery: no asset was included because a non-alarming visual added little beyond the carefully qualified curriculum text.

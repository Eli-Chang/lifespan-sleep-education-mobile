# Clinical review

Status: not clinically reviewed. This build must not be described as validated medical content.

Human clinical review is required for:

- Every pregnancy, maternal, fetal, neonatal, cesarean, heart, birth-weight, preterm-birth, stillbirth, and fetal-growth claim in Modules 3–5.
- Preeclampsia, gestational diabetes, hypertension, respiratory distress, organ damage, and placenta statements.
- All prevalence and statistics claims, including partner satisfaction, pregnancy rhinitis, GERD, and anxiety.
- All PAP adherence, pressure, humidity, ramp, mask leak, and low-oxygen statements.
- Caffeine dose/timing, bedroom temperature, meal timing, exercise targets, naps, and side-sleeping after 28 weeks.
- Saline, fluticasone, budesonide, Afrin, antacids, H2 blockers, proton pump inhibitors, and all pregnancy-safety language.
- Anxiety, CBT, medication, alcohol, marijuana, and mental-health escalation wording.
- Libido, hormones, blood flow, erectile dysfunction, intimacy, and relationship outcome claims.
- Every answer key that may change medical interpretation.

Meaningful readability adaptations and omissions are listed in `CONTENT_REVIEW.md`. New copy is limited to navigation, progress, supportive neutral feedback, privacy/offline explanation, and the educational disclaimer.

Disclaimer used in the app:

> This app provides general educational information and does not replace advice from your healthcare professional. Talk with your healthcare team about questions related to your symptoms, diagnosis, pregnancy, or treatment.

# App safety rules

- Do not log secrets, full learner free text, or real health information.
- Keep consent before AI network calls and preserve reset scope.
- AI is education, not diagnosis, individualized treatment, medication advice, or emergency assessment.
- Preserve CIAS/source wording unless explicitly authorized.
- Use safe fallbacks for malformed, unavailable, or out-of-scope AI responses.
- Browser emulation must never be reported as native testing.

# App safety rules

- Do not log secrets, full learner free text, or real health information.
- The app has no AI companion and makes no network calls; keep it that way unless an authorized change adds one. Preserve reset scope.
- Content is education, not diagnosis, individualized treatment, medication advice, or emergency assessment.
- Preserve CIAS/source wording unless explicitly authorized.
- Any change to learner-visible text follows the `humanizer` and `sleep-app-content-quality` skills.
- Browser emulation must never be reported as native testing.
